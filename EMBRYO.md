# Embryo HDL Spinal

Embryo HDL Spinal is Embryo-Zero's fork of [SpinalHDL](https://github.com/SpinalHDL/SpinalHDL) (v1.15.0, commit 05a01af). It lets people write chips in Scala (SpinalHDL).
It is one of the Embryo chip tools, with Embryo Router (our Freerouting fork) and Embryo PCB (our KiCad fork).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE, LICENSE_core, LICENSE_lib) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
